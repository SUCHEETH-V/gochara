Three filters stand between a transit and its result: Vedha, Vipareeta Vedha and Moorthi Nirnaya. Work them here rather than memorising them cold.

The rule, in three moves

Every beginner error in Gochara comes from stopping after move one.

Move one

Count from the Moon

The Moon's birth rāśi is house 1. Count clockwise through the fixed signs. Gochara is judged from Janma Rāśi, never from the Lagna.

Move two

Name the promise

Each graha has its own favourable houses. Śani in the 3rd, 6th or 11th promises relief. Guru in the 2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th or 11th promises increase.

Move three

Check the paired house

If another graha occupies it, the result is cancelled — the good in Vedha, the bad in Vipareeta Vedha. Occupancy only, never aspect.

One table, read two ways

Left to right it gives Vedha: favourable house → blocker. Right to left it gives Vipareeta Vedha: a graha in the favourable house relieves the difficult one. There is no second table to learn.

Kuja and Śani share an identical table. Rāhu and Ketu are read on the same pattern by most teachers.

Vipareeta Vedha — when the obstruction is a mercy

Vipareeta means reversed. The mechanism does not change at all; only the thing being cancelled changes.

Vedha — a good result is cancelled

The graha stands in a favourable house. Another graha occupies the vedha house. The promised benefit does not arrive.

Vipareeta Vedha — a bad result is cancelled

The graha stands in an unfavourable house. Another graha occupies the paired house. The threatened difficulty does not arrive.

Worked example — relief inside Sāḍe Sātī

Śani transiting the 12th from the Moon is hard: expense, sleeplessness, the closing stretch of Sāḍe Sātī. Śani's table pairs the 3rd with the 12th.

So if Śukra is transiting the 3rd from the Moon at that time, the 3rd strikes the 12th. The pressure is still felt, but the event Śani was set to deliver does not fully materialise.

A benefic in the paired house blocks a malefic result. A malefic there blocks a benefic result. The obstruction does not care which it cancels.

Do not oversell it

Vipareeta Vedha softens a transit. It does not undo what the Daśā has ordained, and it is no licence to give a frightened client false comfort.

Moorthi Nirnaya — the metal a graha wears

Every graha enters a rāśi in one of four forms. The murti it assumes at ingress sets the quality of everything it gives for the whole of that transit.

Gold
Swarṇa Mūrti

Full auspicious result. What the house promises arrives with strength and ease.

Silver
Rajata Mūrti

Good, but reduced. The result comes — after effort, or later than expected.

Copper
Tāmra Mūrti

Mixed and ordinary. Gain mingled with strain; the result is partial at best.

Iron
Loha Mūrti

Adverse. The graha gives difficulty even from an otherwise favourable house.

Why the ingress nakṣatra never changes

Zero degrees of every rāśi falls inside a fixed nakṣatra, so the ingress star is the same every time — only the Janma Nakṣatra you count from differs. Read the sequence aloud once and most students have it by the third repetition:

1 · 3 · 5 · 7 · 10 · 11 · 14 · 16 · 19 · 20 · 23 · 25

Name the variation

Some teachers count instead to the nakṣatra ruling the day of ingress rather than the one the graha itself occupies. Teach one method consistently and tell students which you are using, so they are not thrown when they meet the other in a book.

Reading murti and vedha together

Neither filter overrides the other. Read the house first, then the murti, then the vedha.

MurtiVedhaWhat to expect
Swarṇa / RajataNoneThe transit delivers. This is the combination worth acting on.
Swarṇa / RajataPresentA strong promise held back — often delayed rather than denied. Watch for it once the blocking graha moves on.
Tāmra / LohaNoneA weak or adverse result arrives unhindered. The hardest of the four to sit through.
Tāmra / LohaPresentLittle happens either way. A flat stretch — and for a difficult house, a quiet mercy.

This synthesis is a teaching aid, not a quoted sūtra. The classical texts give murti and vedha separately; combining them is the reader's judgement.

Before you use any of this on a chart

These are filters, not forecasts.

  • The Daśā–Bhukti decides whether an event is on the table at all. A transit cannot deliver what the daśā has not promised.
  • Check natal strength first. A debilitated graha keeps a weak promise even from a favourable house wearing gold.
  • Cross-check Aṣṭakavarga. A transit through a rāśi with high bindus behaves very differently from one with two or three.
  • Vedha is occupancy — a graha physically standing in the paired rāśi. Not aspect. Not lordship.
  • Ravi–Śani and Chandra–Budha never obstruct each other. Any other graha in that house still does.
  • Śukra's pairs in Phaladīpikā are not perfectly reciprocal, and murti counting has a rival method. Teach the received version and name the variation rather than hiding it.
