Vedha — obstruction of the free flow of results

A graha transiting certain houses gives auspicious results. Those results are obstructed by some other graha transiting at some other place. Both houses are counted from the Natal Moon.

GrahaAuspicious transitVedha position

Two exemptions

The Sun does not suffer Vedha due to Saturn, and vice versa. The Moon does not suffer Vedha due to Mercury, and vice versa. Any other graha in that house still obstructs — the exemption belongs to the pair, not the house.

Memory hooks

Mars and Saturn have identical tables. Rahu and Ketu follow the Sun exactly. Vedha is occupancy — a graha physically standing in that house. Not aspect, not lordship.

Source: Phaladeepika

Vipareetha Vedha — the reference point changes

This is the single most common error in the whole topic. The ashubha position is counted from the Moon; the nullifying position is counted from the transiting graha itself.

Ashubha Gochara position — from the Natal Moon

Where the graha stands relative to Janma Rashi tells you whether it threatens an in-auspicious result at all.

Shani in the 12th from the Moon → ashubha

Vipareetha Vedha position — from the transiting graha

Now stop counting from the Moon. Take the rashi the afflicting graha itself occupies as house 1, and count from there.

3rd, 6th or 11th from Shani → relief

GrahaIn-auspicious transit · from Janma RashiVipareetha Vedha · from the transiting graha

How to read it

If the graha occupies one of its in-auspicious houses from the Moon, and any other transiting graha occupies one of the vipareetha vedha houses counted from that graha, the native does not experience the in-auspicious result.

Use the Vipareetha filter in the chakra above — it draws both charts side by side so the renumbering is visible.

Source: Laws of Nature

Nakshatra Vedha — cancellation by star

The results of a graha transiting the 27 nakshatras get cancelled by some other graha occupying certain specified nakshatras, counted from the nakshatra the first graha occupies.

Graha in transitVedha nakshatras · counted from itVedha-causing grahas

Worked example — the Moon in Ashwini

Count from Ashwini. The 7th nakshatra is Punarvasu; the 12th is Uttara Phalguni. If Mars moves into Punarvasu, or the Sun into Uttara Phalguni, the good effects expected from the Moon's transit of Ashwini are nullified.

Note that the vedha-causing graha is specified. Any graha will not do — for the Moon it must be Mars or the Sun.

Moorthi Nirnaya — the metal a graha wears

Every graha enters a rashi in one of four forms, and the murti sets the quality of everything it gives for the whole of that transit.

Gold
Swarna Murti

Full auspicious result. What the house promises arrives with strength and ease.

Silver
Rajata Murti

Good, but reduced. The result comes — after effort, or later than expected.

Copper
Tamra Murti

Mixed and ordinary. Gain mingled with strain; the result is partial at best.

Iron
Loha Murti

Adverse. The graha gives difficulty even from an otherwise favourable house.

Why the ingress nakshatra never changes

Zero degrees of every rashi falls inside a fixed nakshatra, so the ingress star is always the same — only the Janma Nakshatra you count from differs.

These are filters, not forecasts

  • The Dasha–Bhukti decides whether an event is on the table at all. A transit cannot deliver what the dasha has not promised.
  • Check natal strength first. A debilitated graha keeps a weak promise even from an auspicious house wearing gold.
  • Cross-check Ashtakavarga. A transit through a rashi with high bindus behaves very differently from one with two or three.
  • Mind the reference point. Vedha counts from the Moon. Vipareetha Vedha counts from the transiting graha. Nakshatra Vedha counts from its nakshatra.
  • Where sources differ — say so. Venus's Vedha pairs are not perfectly reciprocal, and Moorthi counting has a rival method.
