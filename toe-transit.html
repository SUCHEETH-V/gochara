Vedha

Vedha means obstruction of free flow of results. Planets while transiting in certain houses give auspicious results. These auspicious results are obstructed by some other planet transiting at some other place. Like Shubha Gochara, Vedha positions are also considered from the Natal Moon.

Transiting planetAuspicious transitVedha positions

Exemptions

Sun does not suffer Vedha due to Saturn, and vice versa. Moon does not suffer Vedha due to Mercury, and vice versa. Any other planet in that house still obstructs.

Memory hooks

Mars and Saturn are identical. Rahu / Ketu follow the Sun exactly. Vedha is occupancy — never aspect, never lordship.

Source : Phaladeepika

Vipareetha Vedha

The obstruction of in-auspicious results of a transiting planet in Ashubha Gochara by some other transiting planet at some other place. The native will not experience the in-auspicious results, as they are nullified.

Ashubha Gochara position

Reckoned from the Natal Moon. Tells you whether an in-auspicious result is threatened at all.

Vipareetha Vedha position

Reckoned from the transiting planet itself. Take the Rashi that planet occupies as house 1, and count from there.

Nakshatra Vedha

The results of planets during their transit through the 27 Nakshatras get cancelled by some other planet occupying certain specified Nakshatras, considered from specified positions.

PlanetVedha transits in NakshatrasVedha causing planets

Example — the Moon in Ashwini

Counting from Ashwini, the 7th Nakshatra is Punarvasu and the 12th is Uttara Phalguni. If Mars moves into Punarvasu, or the Sun into Uttara Phalguni, the good effects expected from the Moon's transit of Ashwini are nullified.

Note that the Vedha-causing planet is specified. Any planet will not do.

Moorthy Nirnaya System

Based on Janma Rashi, with reference to the transiting Moon at the time of ingress of a planet (Graha Sankramana) into a new Rashi. The Moon's position on that day, counted from the Natal Moon, sets the nature of that planet's transit results.

Worked example

The Sun is lord of the 5th and posited in the 3rd natally. In transit it is in the 10th from Janma Rashi — Shubha Gochara. But when the Sun ingressed, the Moon stood in the 4th from Janma Rashi: Loha Moorthy. The auspicious transit is modified, and because the Sun rules the 5th, the in-auspicious turn is read with respect to progeny.

A Shubha Gochara can still disappoint. Always check the Moorthy in force at the moment of ingress.

Before you use any of this on a chart

  • The Dasha–Bhukti decides whether an event is on the table at all. A transit cannot deliver what the dasha has not promised.
  • Check natal strength first, and cross-check the Sarvashtakavarga bindus of the Rashi being transited.
  • Vedha is occupancy — a planet physically standing in that house. Not aspect, not lordship.
  • Name the reference point out loud, every time. That single habit prevents almost every error in this topic.
