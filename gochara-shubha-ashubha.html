The rule, in three moves

Every beginner error in Gochara comes from stopping after move one.

Move one

Anchor to Janma Rāśi

The sign occupied by the natal Moon is the Janma Rāśi; its constellation is the Janma Nakṣatra. Every house below is counted from this point — never from the Lagna.

Move two

Read the mind, not just the event

Lagna shows what objectively happens. The Moon shows how the native subjectively experiences it — anxious success is as real a result as a quiet failure.

Move three

Sync with Daśā

Vimśottari Daśā is calculated from the Moon's Nakṣatra. Because Gochara and Daśā share one reference point, both can be read together to time an event precisely.

Worked example

Sun is 5th-house lord, posited in the 3rd house natally. In transit, Sun sits in the 10th house from Janma Rāśi — Śubha Gochara. Lord of Progeny (5th), transiting a gainful house, may indicate an addition to the family.

Moorthi Nirnaya — the metal a graha wears

Even a Śubha Gochara is modified by the transiting Moon's position at the moment a graha ingresses (Graha Sankramaṇa) into a new rāśi. The mūrti it assumes sets the strength of everything it gives for that transit.

Transit Under Check — what's next

Even a Śubha Gochara can be blocked or reversed. Topic 3 introduces the checks astrologers apply before finalising a transit verdict.

1
Vedha

Obstruction — a graha in one house blocks or cancels the good result of a graha in another.

2
Vipareeta Vedha

Reverse Vedha — the obstructing relationship works in the opposite direction, softening a bad result.

3

Obstruction reckoned by constellation (Nakṣatra) rather than by house or sign.

Before you use any of this on a chart

These are filters, not forecasts.

  • Gochara is read from the Natal Moon (Janma Rāśi), never from the Lagna.
  • The Daśā–Bhukti decides whether an event is on the table at all. A transit cannot deliver what the daśā has not promised.
  • Check natal strength first. A debilitated graha keeps a weak promise even from a Śubha house.
  • Moorthi Nirnaya can upgrade or downgrade the base Śubha/Aśubha result — always check both together.
  • Vedha and Vipareeta Vedha (Study Sheet 03) can cancel or soften either result. Treat this table as the first filter, not the last.
  • These are classical generalisations. Always weigh them against the specific natal chart — lordship, house strength, and aspects.
